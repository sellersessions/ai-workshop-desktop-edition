## Recommended Price Point

**Target Price:** $29.99 (standard variant) / $32.99 (deep drawer variant)

**Rationale:**
1. Matches premium quality differentiation strategy
2. Above the crowded $20-25 segment
3. Below luxury/bundle territory ($35+)
4. Only 3 competitors with 1,000+ reviews at this price
5. Bambusi proves the price point works ($29.99, 2,200 units/month)

**Acceptable Range:** $27.99 - $32.99

**Price to Avoid:**
- Below $25: Margin death, race to bottom
- Above $35: Limited market, bundle territory
