## Customer Profile

**Who buys in this niche?**

- **Demographics:**
  - 28-55 years old
  - 65% female
  - Middle to upper-middle income ($60k-150k household)
  - Homeowners or long-term renters

- **Psychographics:**
  - Values organization and tidiness
  - Eco-conscious, prefers sustainable materials
  - Willing to pay premium for quality
  - Influenced by home organization content (HGTV, Pinterest, Instagram)
  - May follow Marie Kondo, The Home Edit, or similar

- **Purchase behavior:**
  - Researches before buying (reads reviews)
  - Buys in "projects" - often purchases multiple organizers at once
  - Seasonal buyer (January, spring cleaning)
  - Responds to bundles and sets

- **Cross-category interests:**
  - Kitchen gadgets
  - Home decor
  - Sustainable/eco products
  - Storage and organization

**Target price:** $34.99

**Follow-up products (6-12 months):**
1. Bamboo Utensil Holder - $24.99
2. Bamboo Spice Drawer Insert - $29.99
3. Bamboo Paper Towel Holder - $19.99
4. Bamboo Cutting Board Set - $39.99

**Estimated brand revenue potential:** $85,000-120,000/month at maturity (18-24 months)

### Scenario 2: Launch with Bundle

**Bundle components:**
1. Drawer organizer (3-piece set)
2. Utensil holder
3. Paper towel holder

**Bundle price:** $69.99 (vs $79.97 individual)

**Advantages:** Higher AOV, harder to comparison shop, instant brand perception
**Risks:** Higher inventory cost, bundle logistics, customer may only want one item
