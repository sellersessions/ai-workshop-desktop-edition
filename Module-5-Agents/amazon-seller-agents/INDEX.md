## Installation

### Option 1: Copy to Workshop Project

These agents are ready to use within this workshop project folder.

### Option 2: Install Globally

To use these in every project, ask Claude:

> Copy the agent files in this folder into my global agents folder.

Claude creates `~/.claude/agents` if it needs to, and copies them across.

## Agent Details

### 1. Amazon Trend Researcher
**Best for:** Finding what to sell next

- Market opportunity assessment
- Competitor analysis
- BSR trend tracking
- Product gap identification

**Example:** "Research trending kitchen gadgets under £30 with BSR under 10,000"

---

### 2. Amazon Reddit Scout
**Best for:** Understanding customers

- Pain point discovery
- Product feedback mining
- Community sentiment analysis
- Competitor mentions

**Example:** "Find what people complain about with camping chairs on Reddit"

---

### 3. Amazon Content Creator
**Best for:** Writing listings

- Product titles (SEO optimized)
- Bullet points (benefit-focused)
- Product descriptions
- A+ Content modules
- Brand story

**Example:** "Write a compelling product title and 5 bullets for my bamboo cutting board"

---

### 4. Amazon Growth Hacker
**Best for:** Scaling sales

- Launch strategies
- PPC campaign planning
- External traffic tactics
- Ranking optimization

**Example:** "Create a 30-day launch plan for my new yoga mat"

---

### 5. Amazon TikTok Strategist
**Best for:** Diversifying channels

- TikTok Shop setup guidance
- Viral content ideas
- Influencer partnership strategy
- Cross-platform traffic

**Example:** "Plan 10 TikTok video ideas for my skincare product"

---

### 6. Amazon Analytics Reporter
**Best for:** Data-driven decisions

- Sales performance reports
- PPC metrics analysis
- Inventory forecasting
- Profitability calculations

**Example:** "Analyse this month's sales data and identify trends"

---

### 7. Amazon Feedback Analyst
**Best for:** Product improvement

- Review sentiment analysis
- Feature request identification
- Quality issue detection
- Competitor review mining

**Example:** "Summarise the top 5 complaints from these 100 reviews"

---

### 8. Amazon Support Responder
**Best for:** Customer communication

- Buyer message templates
- Return/refund responses
- Negative feedback handling
- Seller metric protection

**Example:** "Draft a professional response to this angry customer about late delivery"
