# Pre-Deployment Questionnaire

**Complete this before running the Amazon Product Research Pipeline**

---

## Why This Matters

Running the full pipeline uses significant tokens and time. Answering these questions first:
- Saves you from researching the wrong category
- Helps the pipeline focus on YOUR specific situation
- Flags potential issues BEFORE spending time on research

### 3. Budget Reality Check

**What's your budget for the FIRST inventory order?**

```
[ ] Under $2,000
    - Limited options, small MOQs, domestic suppliers likely
    - Higher risk due to thin margins on small orders

[ ] $2,000 - $5,000
    - Standard starting point
    - Can negotiate basic MOQs with overseas suppliers
    - May need to start with one product

[ ] $5,000 - $10,000
    - Good flexibility
    - Can order samples + first inventory
    - Possible to test 2 products

[ ] $10,000+
    - Full flexibility
    - Better pricing on larger MOQs
    - Can build proper brand ecosystem from start
```

**Important:** This is just inventory. You'll also need PPC budget (typically 30-60 days of ad spend to launch).

### 4. Supplier Preference

**Where are you willing to source products?**

```
[ ] Overseas suppliers (Alibaba, 1688, etc.)
    - Lower cost
    - Higher MOQs
    - Longer lead times (30-60 days)
    - Shipping complexity

[ ] US domestic only
    - Higher cost (often 2-3x)
    - Lower MOQs
    - Faster restocking
    - Simpler logistics

[ ] Open to both
    - Most flexibility
    - Pipeline will analyze both options
```

**Why this matters:** Landed cost calculations change dramatically based on supplier origin.

## Quick Red Flag Check

Before proceeding, confirm you're NOT in these situations:

```
[ ] I'm NOT trying to compete directly with Amazon Basics or major brands
[ ] I'm NOT looking at a product with obvious patent/trademark issues
[ ] I'm NOT rushing because someone told me a "hot product tip"
[ ] I DO have capital beyond just inventory (for PPC, samples, mistakes)
[ ] I UNDERSTAND this is a business, not a get-rich-quick scheme
```

**If any of the above are unchecked, reconsider before proceeding.**
