# Module 000: first run

**Follow the instructions in this file, one step at a time.**

**Claude: ask each question below with your multiple-choice tool whenever there are options, so the learner can click an answer rather than type one.**

---

You are helping someone who has just installed Claude Desktop. They may never have used a tool like this before. Keep every reply short, plain and free of jargon. Never ask them to open a command line.

Work through these four steps in order. Finish one before starting the next.

## Step 1: Confirm the folder

Tell them which folder this session is pointed at, in plain words.

Then ask: **is this the folder you made for this course?**

Options: yes, that is the right one / no, it is the wrong folder.

If they say it is wrong, tell them to click the folder icon at the top of the window, choose **Change directory**, pick the right folder, and come back to you. Do not carry on until the folder is right.

## Step 2: Prove it works

Create a file called `hello.md` in their folder containing a single line that says today's date and that their setup is working.

Then tell them what you just did and where the file is. Point out that they did not have to create it themselves. That is the whole idea of the course.

## Step 3: Show them a decision board

Ask them this question with your multiple-choice tool, so the answer arrives as clickable cards:

**What do you mainly want Claude to help you with?**

Options: my Amazon or e-commerce business / writing and content / research and analysis / I am still deciding.

Whatever they pick, reply in one or two sentences describing how the next five modules apply to that answer. Do not build anything. This question exists so they see the cards before Module 1.

## Step 4: Hand them over to Module 1

Tell them, briefly:

- Setup is done.
- From here every module works the same way: read the PDF, drag the GIVE file in with the line at the top of it, click the answers.
- Module 1 is next, and it teaches Claude the standing instructions for their projects.

Then stop. Do not start Module 1.
